# Change 1: Rewrite the professional introduction

## Why

The first version of the site opened with a factual summary, my program, my graduation year, and my previous employer. It was accurate but it did not say anything. A recruiter would learn where I have been and nothing about what I am good at or what I am looking for.

## Goal

Replace the Home page introduction with a short positioning statement that says what I do, what I bring to it, and what I am moving toward, kept to roughly three sentences.

## What will change

`index.md` intro paragraph, and `about.md` expanded to a fuller version of the same story.

## What will not change

Layout, navigation, CSS, and every other page, so the diff stays readable.

## How I will check it

Preview at 375px and 1280px, click every navigation link, and confirm no invented claims.
