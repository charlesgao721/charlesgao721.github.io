# Change 2: Add detail to the Work Experience page

## Why

The first version listed my employers, titles, and dates and nothing else. That tells a reader where I worked but not what I actually did, and for a portfolio aimed at recruiting the specifics are the entire point.

## Goal

Expand the Work Experience page so each role carries concrete examples, grouped by the kind of work rather than listed as one long run of bullets, and kept scannable because this is a website and not a resume.

## What will change

`work-experience.md` gains grouped detail under the KPMG role and a short description under the current internship.

## What will not change

Layout, navigation, CSS, and the Home, About, and Contact pages.

## How I will check it

Preview at 375px and 1280px since this page gets much longer, confirm headings nest correctly for accessibility, and confirm every figure matches my resume exactly with nothing rounded up or invented.
